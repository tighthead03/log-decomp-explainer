Volume grew 7.5% (+11,345) from 2024 to 2025. The biggest boost came from 90-day supply mandate (Harbor Medicaid patients) (+2,354, from Jun 2025), followed by referrals increased (Northeast region) (+1,759, from Feb 2025), sales / marketing push (Stelara) (+1,230, from Jan 2025). The biggest drags were referrals from Lakeside Dermatology Associates fell 100% (from Jul 2025) (-2,066, from Jul 2025) and intake processing delays (-762, from Oct 2025). Taken together, the specific events below account for +3,217. The remaining +8,128 is underlying growth that isn't tied to any one event (market growth and momentum from patients already on therapy). 1 change found in the data is not in the change log and should be investigated.

How to read the confidence labels

  • High confidence: the effect is clearly different from zero (99.9% level). In 90 simulated tests this label was never given to a fake event.
  • Likely: probably real (95% level) but not certain.
  • Combined effect: two logged events overlap so closely that the data can't split them; reported as one number.
  • Not in change log: investigate: a clear change in the data that no logged event explains.
  • No measurable effect: logged, but the data can't distinguish its impact from zero.
  • The "likely between" range is a 95% interval: the true impact falls inside it about 95% of the time.
